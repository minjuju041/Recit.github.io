In an era of instant uploads and disappearing stories, our deepest personal memories are often reduced to a pixelated blur. We capture everything, yet feel nothing.

Récit kit was born from a desire to reclaim the weight of the moment. Not to share, but to keep. Not to inhabit a screen, but to inhabit a space.

The Sequence

Process 01–03
01 / Directing

Arrival & Shooting

Your Guide Card serves as your slate. Scan to unlock localized audio guides that direct your focus.

02 / Immersion

Sensory Records

Document the invisible layers — the scent of rain, the cadence of voices, the weight of light.

03 / Sealing

Post-travel Finish

Seal it with heavy wax, locking the narrative away. It will be returned to you in one year — a film from your past self.

Key Features

P/C

Camera & Guide Card

We provide guide cards to assist travelers on how to record their trip, and by following the instructions on the cards, you can capture your travel memories in photos.

S/A

Texture & Scent Note

We provide travel journaling sheets similar to wine tasting notes, consisting of a checklist and vocabulary (acidity, body, etc.). Scented candles infused with the scent of the travel destination help you immerse yourself more deeply in your journey.

C/P

Time Capsule Delivery

We are linking a future delivery service where travel memories are perfectly sealed away and returned to you in analog form one year later.
